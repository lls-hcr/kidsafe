1

Install

Two minutes. Nothing else to install: the Privoxy filter is included.

  1. Open KidSafe.dmg. Drag KidSafe onto Applications.
  2. Open KidSafe from Applications.
  3. First time only: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  4. Done. A green shield appears at the top right of the screen.
Good to knowBy default, KidSafe blocks adult content. Everything else is in Settings….
2

The menu bar

Everything starts from the shield, at the top right of the screen.

Status

  • Green: protection on.
  • Orange: protection incomplete. The reason shows, with a button to fix it.

Filtering

The switch pauses filtering. Nothing is deleted. One click resumes it.

Shortcuts

  • Update lists
  • Settings, monitor, this guide
  • Test Privoxy: checks the browser goes through the filter
  • Send feedback, donate, quit
TipTick Open KidSafe when the Mac starts. Protection starts with the session.
3

Block categories

Lists tab. Tick a box, it applies within seconds.

  • Categories: adult, bypass tools (VPNs, proxies), scams, gambling, violence, drugs, dating, social networks, online games, ads.
  • 🐢 Tortoise: very large list. It may slow browsing.
  • Add a list: free lists, a web address or a file on your Mac.
Good to knowBlocking example.com also blocks www.example.com and all its subdomains.
4

Social networks and schedules

Social networks tab. One setting per network: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat…

Allowed
Free access.
Always blocked
Blocked at all times.
Blocked during time slots
Example: at night, 21:00 to 07:00.
Allowed only during time slots
Example: Wednesday and weekends, 14:00 to 18:00.

The badge shows the current state: blocked now or allowed now. When a slot starts, open tabs are cut.

CarefulSchedules are applied by the app. Keep it open: turn on open at startup and a parent password.
5

Block or allow a site

Domains tab. Two columns.

  • Blocked domains: blocked at all times.
  • Allowlist: always allowed, even if a list contains them. Ideal to unblock a site blocked by mistake.

Paste a full address: KidSafe keeps only the domain.

General settings

General tab.

Strict mode
Everything blocked, except the allowlist. For younger children.
List updates
From never to every week. Default: every day.
Parent password
Protects settings, pausing and Quit.
Language
System, Français or English.
PasswordUnlocked for 5 minutes after you enter it. The padlock in the menu locks it again right away.

KidSafe updates

KidSafe checks for new versions every week. It can install them by itself: download, check, relaunch. Your settings stay.

7

The monitor

Menu → Monitor. A small window with key figures.

  • Requests filtered and blocked
  • Domains on lists
  • Networks blocked now
  • Last block

Counters restart from zero when Privoxy restarts.

LightWhen the window is closed, the monitor does nothing.

Detailed view

A local page with charts and tables: blocks per hour and per day, top blocked sites, categories.

Advanced settings

Advanced tab. For experienced users. Parent password required.

Port
Picked automatically if busy. Can be changed by hand.
Privoxy editor
Edit rules in the browser. Closes by itself after 1 hour.
user.action
Your Privoxy rules. Checked before being applied.
Log
Records blocked sites. Open in Console, clear.
Exclusions
Domains that skip the filter (company VPN…).
Troubleshooting
Status, folder, restart or reset Privoxy.
CarefulA mistake here can cut the connection or the filtering.
9

Tips

For solid protection.

  • A standard macOS account for the child, not administrator.
  • A parent password.
  • Open at startup turned on.
  • Bypass tools category ticked: it blocks VPNs and proxies.
  • Firefox: keep Use system proxy settings.
Good to knowA blocked site shows a connection error. That's normal: KidSafe filters by domain, without reading your pages.
10

Common problems

No internet?KidSafe fixes it by itself within seconds. Otherwise: General → Remove proxy, or quit KidSafe.
A site is blocked by mistake?Add it to the allowlist (Domains tab).
A site should be blocked?Reload the page. Then check with Test Privoxy.
An app has no internet after quitting KidSafe?Quit that app and reopen it.
The shield doesn't appear?The menu bar is full. Double-click KidSafe in Applications.
11

Uninstall

Two ways, same result.

  • On the .dmg: double-click Uninstall KidSafe.
  • In the app: General → Uninstall KidSafe…

The filter is removed, settings deleted, the app moved to the Trash.

UpdatingNo need to uninstall. Replace the app, or let KidSafe update itself.
12

Privacy

Your data stays on your Mac.

  • No personal data is sent.
  • Connections: downloading lists and checking GitHub for updates.
  • Log and monitor: on this Mac only.
