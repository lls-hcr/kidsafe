Install

Two minutes. Nothing else to install: the Privoxy filter is included.

  1. Open KidSafe.dmg. Drag KidSafe onto Applications.
  2. Open KidSafe from Applications.
  3. macOS asks to confirm: click Open. KidSafe is signed and checked by Apple.
  4. Done. A green shield appears at the top right of the screen.
Good to knowBy default, KidSafe blocks adult content. Everything else is in Settings….
2

The menu bar

Everything starts from the shield, at the top right of the screen.

Status

  • Green: protection on.
  • Orange: protection incomplete. The reason shows, with a button to fix it.

Filtering

The switch pauses filtering: nothing is blocked, and Privoxy shows “disabled” on http://p.p. Nothing is deleted. One click resumes it.

Disable temporarily: 30 seconds, 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or 1 hour. The menu shows the time left, then filtering resumes on its own. If a parent password is set, it is required.

Shortcuts

  • Update lists
  • Settings, monitor, this guide
  • Test Privoxy: checks the browser goes through the filter
  • Send feedback, donate, quit
TipTick Open KidSafe when the Mac starts. Protection starts with the session.
3

Block categories

Lists tab. Tick a box, it applies within seconds.

  • By topic: adult content, ads and tracking, scams and malware, bypass tools (VPNs, proxies, encrypted DNS). Each topic offers one or more lists; at first, only HaGeZi NSFW is ticked.
  • Level (HaGeZi Pro, HaGeZi Threat Intelligence Feeds): Light, Normal or Strong. The higher the level, the more the list blocks, with a slightly higher chance of blocking a site by mistake.
  • ⓘ: the list's information sheet.
  • 🐢 Tortoise: very large list (full UT1, security at the Strong level). It may slow browsing.
  • Add a list: More topics (gambling, violence, drugs, dating, social networks, online games, illegal downloads, URL shorteners), Other free lists (StevenBlack, EasyList…), a web address or a file on your Mac. Remove an added list with the trash can.
Good to knowBlocking example.com also blocks www.example.com and all its subdomains.

Which list to choose?

The ⓘ sheet shows who maintains the list, what it blocks, its size, how often its author updates it, how often it blocks by mistake, its licence and its address.

HaGeZi
Updated several times a day. Today's reference, with few mistakes: a good choice for every topic.
OISD
Updated every hour. Known for not breaking anything; complements HaGeZi.
UT1
Updated every day by Université Toulouse Capitole, used in French schools. The only one covering topics such as violence or drugs.

Two lists of the same topic complement each other but largely overlap: one is often enough.

Updating lists

Automatic, at the interval chosen in General (counted from the last update, while KidSafe is open), or right away with Update lists. During the update, each list shows its step: downloading, converting to Privoxy's format, replacing in Privoxy.

updated…
Last successful update, with the number of domains added or removed if it changed. Hover: when the content last changed.
In orange
Download failed: the previous copy is still used, so the child stays protected. Hover to see why.

At the bottom of the tab, a summary: lists changed, unchanged, failed. An unchanged list isn't downloaded again: the website just confirms it hasn't changed.

When a site is blocked

What the child sees, and how KidSafe explains it.

Almost all sites are encrypted (https://). The browser then doesn't show KidSafe's page, but its own error message. That's expected: the site is blocked.

BrowserMessage
Firefox“The proxy server is refusing connections”
Chrome, Edge, BraveERR_TUNNEL_CONNECTION_FAILED
Safari“Safari can't open the page”
Unencrypted site (http://)Privoxy's “Request blocked” page

KidSafe's message

At the same time, KidSafe shows the site and the reason in the middle of the screen, whatever the browser. The message goes away at the first click.

Ads and tracking blocked in the background are not reported. The same site is reported at most once every 10 minutes.

Turning the message off

Settings → General → uncheck Show a message when a site is blocked.

Sites stay blocked; only the message goes away. If a parent password is set, it is required.

Block or allow a site

Domains tab. Two columns.

  • Blocked domains: blocked at all times.
  • Allowlist: always allowed, even if a list contains them. Ideal to unblock a site blocked by mistake.

Paste a full address: KidSafe keeps only the domain.

General settings

General tab.

Strict mode
Everything blocked, except the allowlist. For younger children.
List updates
From never to every week. Default: every day.
Parent password
Protects settings, pausing and Quit.
Blocked site
An on-screen message shows the site and the reason. The same site at most once every 10 minutes.
Language
System, Français or English.
PasswordUnlocked for 5 minutes after you enter it. The padlock in the menu locks it again right away.
Forgotten?Click Forgot password? in the password window. The password of a Mac administrator lets you choose a new one, or remove it. Remove Password also offers to turn off the protections added with it.
DefaultsThe Lists, Social networks, Domains and General tabs have a Restore Defaults… button to go back to how they were at first. The password, bypass protection and language don't change.

Protection against workarounds

Offered when you create the parent password, and can be changed later in the General tab. Parent password required.

Cut internet when KidSafe is closed
If KidSafe is closed without the password (open at login turned off, log out, force quit), no site opens, instead of unfiltered access. To restore internet: open KidSafe again, or Quit with the password.
Firefox
Firefox can ignore the Mac's proxy. Lock… installs Firefox's official policy: it always uses the system proxy, for all accounts. macOS asks for the administrator password. Relaunch Firefox; the policy shows at about:policies.
Several accountsThe proxy applies to the whole Mac. With Cut internet, an account where KidSafe isn't open has no internet while the proxy is set. Open KidSafe in that account too, or remove the proxy in System Settings → Network → Wi-Fi → Details → Proxies.

KidSafe updates

KidSafe checks for new versions every week. It can install them by itself: download, check, relaunch. Your settings stay.

LightWhen the window is closed, the monitor does nothing.

A local page with charts and tables: blocks per hour and per day, top blocked sites, categories, events.

EventsKidSafe notes when it was stopped without using Quit (force quit, crash, power cut), and when opening at login was turned off in System Settings. The monitor shows it for a week.

Tips

For solid protection.

  • A standard macOS account for the child, not administrator.
  • A parent password.
  • Open at startup turned on.
  • Bypass tools category ticked: it blocks VPNs and proxies.
  • Cut internet when KidSafe is closed (section 7).
  • Firefox: proxy locked (section 7).
  • A look at the monitor now and then: it reports unexpected stops.
Good to knowA blocked site shows a connection error, and KidSafe shows a message (section 4). That's normal: KidSafe filters by domain, without reading your pages.

Limits

KidSafe makes getting around it hard, not impossible. What it doesn't cover, and what to do about it.

LimitWhat to do
Administrator account. The child can undo everything: proxy, settings, uninstall.A standard account for the child. Keep the administrator password to yourself.
VPNs, VPN extensions, Tor. A VPN app already installed, a VPN or proxy extension in Chrome, Edge or Brave, Opera's built-in VPN, Brave's Tor windows, Tor Browser.Bypass tools category: it blocks downloading them and most of these services. macOS Screen Time to limit apps.
Apps that ignore the proxy. Some games and messaging apps connect directly.Screen Time: System Settings → Screen Time → App Limits.
Force quit. Filtering goes on, but social network schedules stay frozen until KidSafe restarts.The monitor reports it. With Cut internet, the child gains nothing from it.
KidSafe's files. Settings are in the user's folder. A savvy child, in that account, can change them.No complete answer today. It takes technical knowledge; the parent password protects the app, not its files.
Other accounts on the Mac. KidSafe filters while it's open in the session.Open KidSafe in each account to protect.
New connection (iPhone over USB, network adapter).Check the shield. If needed: General → Proxy → Set up.
In practiceA standard account, a parent password, the Bypass tools category, Cut internet and a locked Firefox close the most common workarounds.

Common problems

No internet?KidSafe fixes it by itself within seconds. Otherwise: General → Remove proxy, or quit KidSafe.
No internet while KidSafe is closed?That's Cut internet when KidSafe is closed. Open KidSafe: filtering and internet come back.
A site is blocked by mistake?Add it to the allowlist (Domains tab).
A site should be blocked?Reload the page. Then check with Test Privoxy.
What happens when I quit KidSafe?Filtering stops. Privoxy stays on as a simple relay, blocking nothing, until the end of the session: apps that were using it (Claude, VS Code…) keep internet. KidSafe takes it back when it starts.
The shield doesn't appear?The menu bar is full. Double-click KidSafe in Applications.

Uninstall

Two ways, same result.

  • On the .dmg: double-click Uninstall KidSafe.
  • In the app: General → Uninstall KidSafe…

The filter is removed, settings deleted, the app moved to the Trash. The parent password is required; if it's forgotten, a Mac administrator's password is enough.

UpdatingNo need to uninstall. Replace the app, or let KidSafe update itself.
